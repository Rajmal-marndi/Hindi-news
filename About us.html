About Hindi News

Hindi News ek independent news aggregation aur video streaming application hai. Hamara goal users ko Hindi news videos aur publicly available live news streams ko ek convenient platform par access karne ki suvidha dena hai.

Application ko simple, fast aur user-friendly experience ko dhyan mein rakhkar develop kiya gaya hai.

Hindi News kisi particular news channel ka official application nahi hai.

Our Purpose

Hindi News ka primary purpose users ko available news video content aur live news streams discover aur watch karne mein help karna hai.

  • Hindi news videos tak easy access
  • Publicly available live news streams
  • Simple aur easy-to-use interface
  • Fast news content discovery

Third-Party News Content

Hindi News third-party news channels aur YouTube par publicly available videos/live streams ko display ya play kar sakta hai.

Videos, channel names, logos, trademarks aur other content respective owners ki property hain.

Hindi News kisi third-party news organization ke saath affiliated, sponsored ya officially endorsed nahi hai, jab tak explicitly mention na kiya gaya ho.

No Login Required

Hindi News use karne ke liye user ko account create karne ya login karne ki zarurat nahi hai.

No Advertisements

Hindi News application mein currently advertisements display nahi kiye jaate hain.

Independent Application

Hindi News ek independent application hai. Hum kisi news channel ke editorial decisions ya published content ko control nahi karte.

News content ki accuracy aur editorial responsibility respective content publishers ki hoti hai.
